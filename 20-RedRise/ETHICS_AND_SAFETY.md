# Ethics, Safety, and Guardrails

## Medical Disclaimer (Phase 1)

> **🚨 WARNING**: RedRise provides educational and informational content only. It does **not** diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
> 
> - Always consult a **licensed healthcare professional** (doctor, TCM practitioner, or pharmacist) before starting any herbal supplement.
> - Do not stop or change prescribed SSRIs/SNRIs without speaking to your psychiatrist.
> - If you are experiencing a medical emergency (chest pain, suicidal thoughts, stroke symptoms), call your local emergency number immediately.

## Pastoral Disclaimer (Phases 2 & 3)

> **🕊️ NOTE**: RedRise is a digital companion, not a substitute for pastoral care, church community, or professional Christian counseling.
> 
> - The AI does **not** speak with divine authority.
> - It facilitates reflection and prayer but **never** claims to be Jesus, the Holy Spirit, or a messenger of God.
> - For deep trauma, abuse, or severe mental health crises, seek a qualified human counselor.

---

## Crisis Resources

RedRise will actively detect red‑flag phrases (e.g., "I want to die", "no hope"). When detected, it will immediately present a card with:

- **National Suicide Prevention Lifeline (US)**: 988
- **Crisis Text Line**: Text HOME to 741741
- **International**: A link to find local resources (via `lib/safety/emergency-numbers.ts`)

---

## Data Privacy

- **Phase 1** (Dify): Only anonymized symptom queries. No PII (Personally Identifiable Information).
- **Phases 2 & 3**: All conversations are stored **locally** (in the user's browser IndexedDB or on the local server). No data is sent to external servers.
- **User Control**: Users can delete their entire journey history at any time via the Settings view.

---

## Refusal Rules

The AI must refuse (with a polite "I cannot help with that") requests for:
- Illicit drug manufacturing.
- Replacing medical appointments.
- Guarantees of healing.
- Prophetic future predictions.
