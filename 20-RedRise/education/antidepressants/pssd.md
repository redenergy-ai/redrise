# Post-SSRI Sexual Dysfunction (PSSD)

PSSD is a condition where sexual dysfunction persists after stopping SSRI or SNRI antidepressants.

---

## What Is PSSD?

PSSD stands for **Post-SSRI Sexual Dysfunction**. Symptoms include:

- Loss of libido (low or absent sexual desire)
- Erectile dysfunction (difficulty achieving or maintaining erection)
- Delayed or absent orgasm
- Reduced genital sensation
- Emotional numbness affecting intimacy

---

## How Common Is It?

- **58%–73%** of people on SSRIs experience sexual dysfunction while on the drug
- A significant portion of these individuals **do not recover** after stopping
- The exact prevalence of persistent PSSD is unknown, but it is underreported and understudied

---

## Why Does It Happen?

SSRIs affect serotonin receptors, many of which are involved in sexual function. Chronic use can lead to **persistent receptor changes** that do not reverse when the drug is stopped. Other mechanisms include:

- Hormonal disruption (e.g., decreased testosterone, prolactin elevation)
- Oxidative stress
- Epigenetic changes

---

## Emotional and Relational Impact

- **Loss of identity** – sexuality is core to who we are
- **Relationship strain** – intimacy becomes difficult or impossible
- **Grief** – mourning the loss of one's sexual self
