# Common Side Effects of Antidepressants

SSRIs and SNRIs can cause a wide range of side effects. Some are temporary, but others persist for the duration of use or even after stopping.

---

## Physical Side Effects

| Side Effect | Frequency |
| :--- | :--- |
| Weight gain | Common |
| Drowsiness / fatigue | Very common |
| Dry mouth | Very common |
| Dizziness | Common |
| Nausea / gastrointestinal distress | Common (especially at startup) |
| Headaches | Common |
| Sweating / night sweats | Common |
| Tremor | Less common |

---

## Emotional Side Effects

| Side Effect | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Emotional blunting** | A feeling of being "numb" or disconnected from emotions |
| **Apathy** | Lack of motivation, disinterest in previously enjoyed activities |
| **Irritability** | Increased sensitivity, frustration |
| **Depression recurrence** | The drug may stop working, requiring dose increases or switching |

---

## Cognitive Side Effects

| Side Effect | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Brain fog** | Difficulty concentrating, mental "sludge" |
| **Memory issues** | Short-term memory impairment |
| **Word‑finding difficulty** | Trouble finding words or articulating thoughts |

---

## Sexual Side Effects

- See the dedicated **[PSSD page](/education/antidepressants/pssd.md)** for detailed information
- Sexual dysfunction affects **58%–73%** of people on SSRIs
- This can include loss of libido, erectile dysfunction, delayed or absent orgasm
- These symptoms can persist after stopping the drug

---

## Long-Term Side Effects

- Increased risk of developing type 2 diabetes
- Bone density loss (increased fracture risk)
- Cardiovascular issues (QT prolongation, increased stroke risk)
- Persistent withdrawal symptoms upon cessation
