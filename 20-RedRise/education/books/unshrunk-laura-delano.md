# *Unshrunk* by Laura Delano – Book Summary

**Published:** March 2025

**Description:** *Girl, Interrupted* for the age of the prescription pill. Laura Delano was prescribed 19 different medications starting as a teenager and spent 14 years heavily medicated before successfully tapering off.

**Links:**
- Book: https://unshrunkthebook.com
- Inner Compass Initiative: https://www.theinnercompass.org
- Laura's website: https://www.lauradelano.com

---

## Part 1: The Beginning (Age 13)

At 13, Laura Delano was put on her first psychiatric drug for what was, in hindsight, mere teen angst. Within months, she was diagnosed with bipolar disorder and started on a "cascade of prescriptions"—one drug to counteract the side effects of another.

---

## Part 2: The Cascade (19 Drugs, 14 Years)

What followed was decades of psychiatric drugs given to counteract the effects of other drugs. She quickly became one of the 80% of 59 million Americans on psychiatric meds long‑term. Psych wards, the revolving door, the thousand medicine adjustments.

---

## Part 3: The System

Years of brain‑fogging drugs‑upon‑drugs, punctuated by years of hospitalizations. The system that was supposed to help her only made things worse.

---

## Part 4: The Turning Point

She stumbled into an Alcoholics Anonymous group, which focused on taking responsibility for one's own life. That simple notion—along with the common fellowship of people helping each other without professionals—led her to wonder if her worsening mental health was due not to her lack of response to drugs, but to the drugs themselves.

---

## Part 5: The Taper

Against advice, she began tapering off all of them. As she did, she researched—reading Robert Whitaker's *Anatomy of an Epidemic*, Viktor Frankl's *Man's Search for Meaning*, and Rainer Maria Rilke's poetry.

> *"The words of these men ignited a fire in me to feel, to just sit and feel."*

---

## Part 6: The Recovery

Off all drugs, she had a "holy shit" realization: Her problem really was "the meds" all along. She founded Inner Compass Initiative, a nonprofit that helps people make informed choices about psychiatric drugs and withdrawal.

> *"I don't need to 'figure myself out,' to force a change in my day‑to‑day reality. I trust fully in my own process—in this intelligence within me, within each and every one of us…that sits deeper than thought, that knows where to take us each from here."*

> *"We're built for tribes and villages and neighborhoods and potluck dinners. We're meant to feel it all, and bear it all, together."*
