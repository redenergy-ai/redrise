## Who He Is

Dr. Nicholas Fabiano is a psychiatry resident and researcher at the University of Ottawa. He is emerging as a leading voice challenging the foundations of modern mental health care.

## Focus Areas

- Lifestyle psychiatry – exercise, nutrition, sleep, and supplements (like creatine) for mental health
- Challenging the serotonin hypothesis – questioning the "chemical imbalance" narrative
- SSRI overprescription and systemic barriers to holistic care
- Exercise as a treatment – comparable to or exceeding antidepressants
- Inflammation, mitochondrial dysfunction, and microplastics in mental health
