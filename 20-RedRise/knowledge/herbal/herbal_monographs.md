# Natra-Heal Product Monographs

> **Disclaimer:** This information is for educational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare practitioner before starting any supplement regimen, especially if you are pregnant, nursing, taking prescription medication, or have an underlying medical condition. These statements have not been evaluated by SAHPRA.

## Dopamine Assist Capsules

- **Primary Use:** Aggression, anxiety, depression, fatigue, lethargy, mood disorders, apathy. Helps reduce sugar, caffeine, tobacco, and amphetamine cravings.
- **Key Ingredient:** L-Tyrosine – 325mg per capsule.
- **Dosage:** As directed on label.
- **Mechanism:** L-Tyrosine is a precursor to dopamine, norepinephrine, and epinephrine. Supports motivation, focus, and mood regulation.
- **Contraindications:** Caution with MAOIs, thyroid medication, or in individuals with melanoma history.
- **Evidence Level:** Traditional use; L-Tyrosine has RCT support for cognitive performance under stress.

## Depression Assist

- **Key Ingredients:** L-Glutamine, L-Phenylalanine, L-Tyrosine (156mg each), 5-HTP/Tryptophan (35mg), P5P (8mg), Vitamin C (156mg).
- **Primary Use:** Mood support, stress reduction, emotional balance.
- **Dosage:** 1 capsule with dinner (personalised protocol).
- **Mechanism:** 5-HTP is a direct precursor to serotonin; L-Glutamine supports GABA production; L-Tyrosine provides dopamine precursor.
- **Contraindications:** Caution with SSRIs, SNRIs, or MAOIs (serotonin syndrome risk). Avoid if using other serotonergic agents.
- **Evidence Level:** 5-HTP has RCT support for depression and anxiety; amino acid blend has traditional use.

## Serotonin 60 Vegicaps

- **Primary Use:** Mood balance, relaxation, healthy sleep.
- **Key Ingredients:** 5-HTP (Griffonia Seed Extract), Vitamin B6, Magnesium, Rhodiola Rosea or Ashwagandha.
- **Dosage:** 1–2 capsules daily, preferably with meals or in the evening.
- **Mechanism:** 5-HTP is a direct precursor to serotonin; B6 supports neurotransmitter synthesis; Magnesium calms the nervous system.
- **Side Effects:** Mild digestive discomfort possible in sensitive individuals.
- **Contraindications:** Not recommended with prescription antidepressants unless advised by a healthcare professional.

## Acetylcholine Assist Capsules

- **Primary Use:** Memory problems, mitochondrial protection, peripheral neuropathies and pain.
- **Key Ingredients:** L-Arginine – 133.5mg, N-Acetyl L-Carnitine – 133.5mg.
- **Dosage:** As directed on label.
- **Mechanism:** N-Acetyl L-Carnitine increases acetylcholine production, supports mitochondrial function, and acts as a mitochondrial antioxidant.
- **Evidence Level:** ALCAR has RCT support for cognitive function and neuroprotection.

## NAC (N-Acetyl Cysteine) 60 Capsules

- **Primary Use:** Liver detoxification, antioxidant support, respiratory health, immune function.
- **Key Ingredient:** N-Acetyl Cysteine – 697mg per capsule.
- **Dosage:** As directed on label; typical 600mg daily.
- **Mechanism:** Precursor to glutathione (master antioxidant); modulates glutamate; supports detoxification pathways.
- **Side Effects:** GI upset, headaches (common at initiation due to detox mobilisation); transient rash possible.
- **Contraindications:** May reduce effectiveness of nitroglycerin; caution with certain antibiotics.

## Magnesium Glycinate 60

- **Primary Use:** Muscle and nerve comfort, calmness, nutritional balance.
- **Key Ingredient:** Magnesium Glycinate – 91.2mg per capsule.
- **Dosage:** 1 capsule 1-2 hours before bed (personalised protocol).
- **Mechanism:** Highly absorbable chelated form; glycine acts as inhibitory neurotransmitter, promoting relaxation.
- **Side Effects:** Generally well-tolerated; high doses may cause diarrhoea.
- **Contraindications:** May interfere with absorption of certain antibiotics (tetracyclines, quinolones); separate by 2+ hours.

## Ashwagandha Capsules

- **Primary Use:** Restorative effects; immune support; counters effects of aging and stress; improves learning and memory; reduces anxiety and depression.
- **Key Ingredient:** Withania somnifera / Ashwagandha – 641mg per capsule.
- **Dosage:** 1 capsule 3 times per day.
- **Mechanism:** Adaptogen; reduces cortisol; modulates stress response; supports HPA axis.
- **Side Effects:** Dose-related: drowsiness, stomach upset, loose stools, nausea, headache; rare liver problems.
- **Contraindications:** Caution with sedatives, thyroid medication, immunosuppressants, antihyperglycemics.

## Sleepy Head 50ml

- **Primary Use:** Evening relaxation, restful sleep.
- **Key Ingredients:** Valerian 36%, Passionflower 22%, Hops 14%, Sceletium Toruosum 14%, Skullcap 7%, Chamomile 7%.
- **Dosage:** 20 drops in water about ½ hour before bedtime, or when necessary.
- **Mechanism:** Traditional botanicals that support GABAergic and serotonergic pathways for relaxation.
- **Side Effects:** May cause drowsiness; caution with driving or operating machinery.
- **Contraindications:** Caution with sedatives, alcohol, or CNS depressants.

## Quercetin, Bromelain & Resveratrol 60

- **Primary Use:** Antioxidant support, inflammation reduction, immune support, heart health, allergy relief.
- **Key Ingredients:** Resveratrol 174mg, Quercetin 174mg, Bromelain 87mg, Black Pepper 3.5mg.
- **Dosage:** 1–4 capsules daily.
- **Mechanism:** Antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties; supports immune function and cardiovascular health.
- **Contraindications:** Caution with blood thinners; bromelain may increase bleeding risk.

## GABA 60

- **Primary Use:** Stress reduction, deep relaxation, mood balance.
- **Mechanism:** GABA has an inhibitory effect on overly stimulated neurons. Low GABA levels are linked to anxiety, depression, and insomnia.
- **Dosage:** As directed on label.
- **Contraindications:** Caution with sedatives, alcohol, or CNS depressants.

## Anti-Aging Assist Capsules

- **Primary Use:** Slowing down the ageing process.
- **Key Ingredients:** L-Histidine, Glycine, L-Arginine, L-Ornithine, L-Phenylalanine, L-Tyrosine, GABA, L-Carnitine, Vitamin C, Taurine, Nicotinamide, P5P, B12, Zinc, Selenium, Glutathione.
- **Dosage:** 1 capsule 3 times per day.
- **Mechanism:** Comprehensive amino acid and antioxidant blend supporting cellular health and neurotransmitter balance.
- **Contraindications:** Caution with multiple amino acid supplements; consult healthcare provider.

## Natra-Slim Capsules

- **Primary Use:** Weight loss, appetite reduction, metabolism support.
- **Key Ingredients:** L-Carnitine 197mg, Green Tea 131mg, L-Phenylalanine 131mg, Hoodia Gordonii 66mg, Aloe Ferox 66mg, Zinc, Chromium.
- **Dosage:** 1 capsule 3 times per day, ½ hour before meals.
- **Contraindications:** Caution with stimulants; green tea contains caffeine.
