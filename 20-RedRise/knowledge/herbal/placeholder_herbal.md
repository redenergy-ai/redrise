## TCM Herbs for Anxiety

### Suan Zao Ren (Ziziphus jujuba)
- **Property**: Sweet, sour, neutral
- **Meridians**: Heart, Liver, Gallbladder
- **Actions**: Nourishes Heart yin, calms spirit, relieves palpitations and insomnia
- **Dosage**: 10–30g in decoction
- **Contraindications**: Diarrhea, excess dampness

### Gan Mai Da Zao (Licorice, Wheat, Jujube)
- **Property**: Sweet, neutral
- **Meridians**: Heart, Spleen
- **Actions**: Nourishes Heart yin, calms spirit, relieves irritability
- **Dosage**: 9–30g each
- **Contraindications**: Diabetes, edema

### He Huan Pi (Albizia bark)
- **Property**: Sweet, neutral
- **Meridians**: Heart, Liver, Spleen
- **Actions**: Relieves depression, calms spirit, promotes blood circulation
- **Dosage**: 6–15g
- **Contraindications**: None significant

## Western Supplement Studies
