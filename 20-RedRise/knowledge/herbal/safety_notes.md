# Cautions & Safety Notes

## Herb-Drug Interaction Warnings

| Drug Class | Risk | Natra-Heal Products to Avoid/Use with Caution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| SSRIs / SNRIs | Serotonin syndrome risk | Serotonin, Depression Assist (5-HTP), Sleepy Head (Sceletium) |
| Blood Thinners | Increased bleeding risk | Quercetin/Bromelain/Resveratrol, Ashwagandha |
| Sedatives / CNS Depressants | Additive sedation | Sleepy Head, GABA, Ashwagandha, Magnesium Glycinate |
| Antihypertensives | Additive blood pressure lowering | Ashwagandha, Blood Vessel Formula |
| Antidiabetics | Hypoglycaemia risk | Ashwagandha |

## General Contraindications

- **Pregnancy & Lactation:** Most supplements not tested for safety; avoid unless under medical supervision.
- **Liver or Kidney Disease:** Consult healthcare provider before use.
- **Surgery:** Discontinue herbs/supplements 2 weeks before scheduled surgery.
- **Prescription Medication:** Always consult a health practitioner before taking any supplement.

## When to Seek Medical Help

- Severe or sudden onset symptoms (chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe headache).
- Suicidal thoughts or self-harm ideation.
- Signs of serotonin syndrome: agitation, confusion, rapid heart rate, sweating, tremors.
- Allergic reactions: rash, itching, swelling, difficulty breathing.
- Persistent or worsening side effects.
