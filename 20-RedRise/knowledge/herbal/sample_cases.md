# Sample User Cases

> **Disclaimer:** These cases are educational examples only and do not constitute medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare practitioner before starting any supplement regimen, especially if you are pregnant, nursing, taking prescription medication, or have an underlying medical condition. These statements have not been evaluated by SAHPRA.

## Case 1: Emotional Flatness & Low Motivation

**Profile:** 45-year-old female, post-SNRI withdrawal, experiencing emotional flatness, low motivation, and brain fog.

### Recommended Protocol

- **Morning:** Dopamine Assist (1 capsule) – supports motivation and drive.
- **Evening:** Depression Assist (1 capsule with dinner) – supports mood balance.
- **Bedtime:** Magnesium Glycinate (1 capsule) – for relaxation and sleep.
- **Lifestyle:** Morning sunlight, protein-rich breakfast, regular exercise.

**Follow-up:** Monitor mood, energy, and motivation over 4-6 weeks.

## Case 2: Anxiety & Insomnia

**Profile:** 38-year-old male with work-related stress, difficulty falling asleep, and daytime anxiety.

### Recommended Protocol

- **Morning:** Ashwagandha (1 capsule) – for stress resistance.
- **Evening:** Sleepy Head (20 drops in water before bed) – for relaxation.
- **As needed:** GABA (1 capsule) – for acute stress.
- **Lifestyle:** Meditation, breathing techniques, reduce screen time before bed.

**Follow-up:** Monitor sleep quality and anxiety levels over 4 weeks.

---

## Case 3: Brain Fog & Cognitive Decline

**Profile:** 60-year-old female with memory problems, poor concentration, and mitochondrial fatigue.

### Recommended Protocol

- **Morning:** Acetylcholine Assist (1 capsule) – for memory and mitochondrial support.
- **Morning:** NAC (1 capsule) – for antioxidant support.
- **Lifestyle:** Hydration, brain-healthy foods (Omega-3 rich), regular mental stimulation.

**Follow-up:** Monitor cognitive function and energy levels over 8 weeks.
