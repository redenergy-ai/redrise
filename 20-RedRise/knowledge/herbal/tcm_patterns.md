# Pattern Differentiation Using Natra-Heal Products

> **Disclaimer:** This information is for educational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare practitioner before starting any supplement regimen, especially if you are pregnant, nursing, taking prescription medication, or have an underlying medical condition. These statements have not been evaluated by SAHPRA.

## Low Mood & Emotional Flatness (Dopamine/Serotonin Imbalance)

- **Key Symptoms:** Low motivation, emotional flatness, apathy, fatigue, poor concentration.
- **Recommended Products:** Dopamine Assist – for motivation and drive; Serotonin – for mood balance and sleep; Depression Assist – for combined amino acid support.
- **Lifestyle Advice:** Morning sunlight, regular exercise, protein-rich breakfast.

## Anxiety & Stress (Nervous System Overactivity)

- **Key Symptoms:** Excessive rumination, worrying, uneasiness, apprehension, fear, rapid heartbeat, insomnia.
- **Recommended Products:** Anxiety Remedy – for excessive rumination; Natra-Calm – for nervous system function; Ashwagandha – for stress resistance; GABA – for relaxation.
- **Lifestyle Advice:** Meditation, breathing techniques, reduce exposure to stressful situations.

## Sleep Difficulties (Insomnia, Restlessness)

- **Key Symptoms:** Difficulty falling asleep, waking during the night, restless mind.
- **Recommended Products:** Sleepy Head – valerian, passionflower, hops blend; Magnesium Glycinate – for relaxation; Serotonin – supports sleep.
- **Lifestyle Advice:** Consistent bedtime, reduce screen time before bed, avoid caffeine after 2 PM.

## Brain Fog & Cognitive Support

- **Key Symptoms:** Poor memory, difficulty concentrating, mental fatigue.
- **Recommended Products:** Acetylcholine Assist – for memory and mitochondrial support; NAC – for antioxidant support.
- **Lifestyle Advice:** Hydration, Omega-3 rich foods, adequate sleep.
