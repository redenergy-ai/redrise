# Western Supplement Monographs (Natra-Heal Range)

> **Disclaimer:** This information is for educational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare practitioner before starting any supplement regimen, especially if you are pregnant, nursing, taking prescription medication, or have an underlying medical condition. These statements have not been evaluated by SAHPRA.

## L-Tyrosine (in Dopamine Assist)

- **Mechanism:** Precursor to dopamine, norepinephrine, and epinephrine. Supports motivation, focus, and mood.
- **Dosage:** 325mg per capsule (Dopamine Assist).
- **Evidence:** RCT support for cognitive performance under stress.
- **Contraindications:** Caution with MAOIs, thyroid medication.

## 5-HTP (in Serotonin & Depression Assist)

- **Mechanism:** Direct precursor to serotonin; supports mood, sleep, and appetite regulation.
- **Dosage:** As directed on product labels.
- **Evidence:** 5-HTP (Griffonia Seed Extract) studied for naturally boosting serotonin levels.
- **Contraindications:** Caution with SSRIs, SNRIs, or MAOIs (serotonin syndrome risk).

## N-Acetyl Cysteine (NAC)

- **Mechanism:** Precursor to glutathione; antioxidant; supports detoxification and respiratory health.
- **Dosage:** 697mg per capsule.
- **Evidence:** Systematic reviews support NAC for mood disorders, addiction, and neuroprotection.
- **Contraindications:** May reduce effectiveness of nitroglycerin.

## Magnesium Glycinate

- **Mechanism:** Highly absorbable chelated mineral; supports muscle and nerve comfort.
- **Dosage:** 91.2mg elemental magnesium per capsule.
- **Evidence:** Systematic reviews support magnesium for sleep, anxiety, and stress reduction.
- **Contraindications:** May interfere with antibiotic absorption.

## Ashwagandha

- **Mechanism:** Adaptogen; reduces cortisol; modulates stress response; supports HPA axis.
- **Dosage:** 641mg per capsule, 1 capsule 3 times daily.
- **Evidence:** RCTs support Ashwagandha for stress reduction, anxiety, and cognitive function.
- **Contraindications:** Caution with sedatives, thyroid medication, immunosuppressants.
