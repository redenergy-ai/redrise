# Guided Journey – Experience Jesus. Really.

## Session 1: The Invitation

Jesus says to you: "What do you want me to do for you?" (Mark 10:51)

**Reflect**: 
- What is your deepest desire right now?
- What do you long for Him to touch?
- Are you afraid to ask?

**Prayer**: Lord, help me be honest with You about my deepest longings.

---

## Session 2: The Love of the Father

Read Matthew 3:16‑17. Jesus is baptized and the Father speaks: "This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased."

**Reflect**:
- Can you imagine the Father looking at you and saying the same?
- What lies stop you from believing you are beloved?
- What would change in your life if you fully believed this?

**Prayer**: Father, help me receive Your love as Your beloved child.

---

## Session 3: The Exchange

"You shall know the truth, and the truth shall set you free." (John 8:32)

**Reflect**:
- What burden are you carrying that was never meant for you?
- What lie is exhausting you?
- Exchange your burden for His yoke – His yoke is easy, His burden is light (Matthew 11:28‑30).

**Prayer**: Jesus, I give You my ______. I receive Your peace in exchange.
