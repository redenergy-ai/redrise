# The Pause – Excerpts from John Eldredge

## Breath Prayer

"Breathe in the love of God, breathe out your fear. Breathe in His peace, breathe out your anxiety. Breathe in His presence, breathe out your worry."

## Releasing Control

"Take a moment. Close your eyes. Feel the weight of everything you are carrying – the worries, the demands, the expectations. Now imagine lifting that weight and placing it into the hands of Jesus. He says, 'Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.' Just pause. Just let go. He's got this."

## Presence Check

"Ask yourself: What am I feeling right now? Not what you *should* feel – what do you actually sense? In your body? In your heart? Name it to God. He can handle your honesty."
