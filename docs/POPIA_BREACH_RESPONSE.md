# RedRise POPIA Security Compromise Response

This is an operational placeholder and must be reviewed by the appointed Information Officer and South African privacy counsel before production launch.

## Trigger
Use this protocol when there are reasonable grounds to believe that personal information has been accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person.

## Immediate response
1. Contain the incident and preserve evidence.
2. Identify affected systems, data categories, users, processors/operators, and likely time window.
3. Record when RedRise became aware of the compromise and all response actions.
4. Assess likely harm and implement measures to reduce further exposure.
5. Require any operator/processor involved to provide incident details without delay.

## Notifications
Under POPIA section 22, the responsible party should notify the South African Information Regulator and affected data subjects as soon as reasonably possible after discovering a qualifying security compromise, subject to any lawful delay requested by law enforcement.

Regulator notifications should use the Information Regulator eServices security-compromise process current at the time of the incident.

Affected-user notices should explain, in clear language where known:
- the nature and scope of the compromise;
- the personal information affected;
- likely consequences or risks;
- measures RedRise has taken or will take;
- practical steps the user can take to reduce harm;
- contact details for RedRise's Information Officer/privacy contact.

## SAHPRA
SAHPRA is **not** treated as the universal POPIA breach-notification authority in this protocol. Notify SAHPRA only when the facts of the incident create a separate, applicable medicines/medical-device or other regulatory reporting duty confirmed by the responsible compliance owner or legal counsel.

## Follow-up
- complete root-cause analysis;
- remediate security weaknesses;
- document decisions and evidence;
- review whether processors, access controls, retention rules, or consent flows need changes;
- maintain a breach register and track completion of user/regulator notifications.
